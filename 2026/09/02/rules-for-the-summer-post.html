Six Rules for the Summer: A Review

Sep 2, 2026

Welp, it’s September now, meaning summer (or at least what I like to call summer: May through August) is now over. And that means it’s time to take a look back at how I did following my 6 rules. I was quite successful with some, not quite as much with others. Let’s dive in.

1. Every weekend must have a plan.

Of all my rules, this was probably the most successful. With the list being ranked by importance, this makes sense. Over the summer I did A LOT of stuff:

  • 5 overnight camping trips (Okanagan, Pump Peak, Chilliwack, Tricouni-Berg Lake, Phelix Hut)
  • 4 day hikes (Alouette Mountain, Brunswick/Harvey, Baden Powell (Horseshoe Bay-Grouse), Grouse Grind)
  • 4 50km+ bike rides (Squamish, SFU, White Rock, Bowen Island)

I’d say there were probably only 1 or 2 weekends when I didn’t do anything, and those were near the start, and after I broke my foot mid-August. Overall, great success! This summer has felt so full of adventure, and I’m immensely grateful for that. MASSIVE shout-out to the Varsity Outdoors Club at UBC - probably about half of the things I listed above were hosted by them.

2. Make activities social if possible.

This rule was also quite successful! Compared to the previous year, I can’t think of many times where I went and did a big activity alone. From movies, to (shorter) rides, to parties, I had a great time interacting with my friends (new and old) this summer!

3. No tech (alone) past 9pm.

Now we are moving into territory where I was not quite as successful. I’d say I managed to keep this up about half of the time. For the first half of the summer, I was often waking up around 6:30am or so. I never thought I’d be the guy to do that. Now I just need to become more consistent with it. There was a period around middle-to-late summer where I just completely ditched this rule.

4. Call home at least once a week.

This rule was also moderately successful. There were probably about 2 or 3 weeks where I neither called my parents nor visited home. I will say, I think I preferred the phone calls more, though, for some reason. I guess because the focus was actually on talking to my parents.

5. Call first, then text.

I low-key forgot this rule existed. I mean, I am calling people more now than I used to, but not that much more. I’d say this rule was kind of a failure. But at the same time, I guess this rule was also kind of in place to reinforce making more plans (rule #1) and being more social (rule #2), so my failure to follow this rule wasn’t really that big of a deal.

6. Write at least one blog post a week.

And now we come to the rule I feel most guilty about, because it’s also the one where my failure is most public. I did not write a blog post every week. I did not even come close. In total, I wrote 7 posts over the span of 15 weeks. I missed 8 WEEKS (well, technically 9, because I wrote 2 in one week). Nonetheless, I still wrote a lot more than I would have had I never set this goal, and I also wrote some very important pieces during this time as well, such as my Personal Philosophy series. With that said, I don’t think I really followed this rule. Why? Mainly due to a mixture of laziness and realizing that writing… is actually kind of hard. I’ll be working on this over the next 4 months.
